A LITTLE CLARITY

Seeing double?
Good questions.

Is this one token or two different projects?

Twice plans one name, ticker and image for two independent tokens: a direct PumpSwap pool on Solana and Pons on Robinhood Chain. Each has its own supply and market. Matching market caps is a goal, not a guarantee.

How does the market-cap peg work?

The planned market maker holds tokens and cash on both chains. Your capital is allocated automatically: 50% to Solana liquidity, 25% to Robinhood inventory and 12.5% to cash reserves on each chain. Twice manages this capital. Live trading is not enabled, and matching prices are not guaranteed.

Do I need a wallet on both chains?

Yes, the planned launch needs a Solana wallet and an EVM wallet for Robinhood Chain. You can start the draft and choose your funding amount on either chain. Connect both wallets when reviewing the plan. Connection alone does not move funds.

Can I launch a real token here today?

Use Launch your token to connect both wallets, review funding and launch. These are mainnet transactions, including from localhost. Pons launches first, then Twice creates the PumpSwap pool. Fees and trading losses are real; a completed mainnet test is still pending.
